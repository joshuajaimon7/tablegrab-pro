Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

TableGrab Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is developed by TinyStack Labs. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the TableGrab Pro Google Chrome Extension.

2. Data Collection and Processing

TableGrab Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing information.

  • 100% Local Execution: All table parsing, data extraction, and file formatting (CSV, TSV, JSON) occur entirely on your local device within the Google Chrome browser environment.
  • No Server Transmission: The contents of the webpages you inspect or the tables you export are never transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The extension does not contain third-party trackers, analytics scripts, or telemetry.

3. Permissions Used

  • activeTab: Used solely to access the DOM of the webpage you are currently viewing to detect HTML tables when you click the extension or hover over data.
  • scripting: Used to execute the client-side extraction scripts inside the active browser tab.
  • storage: Used strictly on your local computer to remember your user preferences (e.g. license status).

4. Third-Party Payment Processing

If you choose to purchase a TableGrab Pro license, payment processing is handled securely by certified third-party payment providers (Lemon Squeezy / Stripe). We do not store or process your credit card numbers or financial details.
